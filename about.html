About Vheoa

The world's students, ranked by merit — never by money.

What is Vheoa?

Vheoa is a public leaderboard for students. Every student who submits gets a profile. Visitors browse those profiles, and if they like what they see, they upvote. That's it. Rankings are driven entirely by votes and seniority — no paid placements, no promoted listings, no sponsored rank.

How ranking works

  1. More upvotes wins. A student with more votes always outranks one with fewer.
  2. Seniority breaks ties. When two students have the same number of votes, the one who registered earlier ranks higher.
  3. One vote per student per day. A visitor can vote for a student once every 24 hours (UTC). They can vote for as many different students as they like.

Where your CV lives

Vheoa does not host your CV. You keep it on your own Google Drive, set sharing to "Anyone with the link — Viewer," and give us the link. That way you own your document, can update it whenever you want, and can revoke access at any time.

How we keep votes honest

Every vote is checked against four layers:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — an invisible bot check on every vote.
  • Device fingerprint — a stable hash of browser and hardware signals.
  • IP-based rate limits — 60 votes per network per day.
  • Per-voter limits — 20 votes per day, per fingerprint, with a rotating daily salt for privacy.

If a profile accumulates suspicious votes, we flag it and a moderator reviews it. Votes that fail review are removed. Repeat abusers are shadow-banned.

Reporting a profile

Every profile has a Report button. If a CV is fake, offensive, or simply broken, tell us. Reports are reviewed manually, and after enough independent reports a profile is hidden automatically pending review.

Merchants

A paid directory for student-related merchants will arrive in a later phase. It will be completely separate from the student leaderboard — paid listings will never appear in student rankings.
